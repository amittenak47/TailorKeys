2. model/words_domains.txt and model/words_domains_offensive.txt
Words with entries in English Wiktionary, and words common in the text above.

Source: English Wiktionary, kaikki.org extract (file dated 2026-10-25), by Wiktionary contributors
Source URL: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0) and GNU Free Documentation License

Modified by TailorKeys: only the word is kept, lowercased; listed misspellings, words missing an apostrophe and likely
typos removed; ranked by how often the text above uses each word; offensive words moved to their own file.
These files are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

3. model/words_extra.txt and model/words_offensive.txt
Built from the same wordfreq and AOSP LatinIME sources as the lists in NOTICE-words-extra.txt, with likely misspellings
removed using the list in NOTICE-misspellings.txt. The licenses in those notices apply.

4. model/head_words.txt and model/tokenizer.json
head_words.txt holds the 20,000 listed words the text above uses most. tokenizer.json holds byte-level word pieces
trained by TailorKeys on the text above, with the Hugging Face tokenizers library (Apache License, Version 2.0).

5. model/step.int8.onnx
The autocorrect model, trained by TailorKeys on the text in section 1 and typing simulated from it. The sources' licenses and
attribution above apply to it as far as they reach a trained model. TailorKeys makes no claim that they do not.
